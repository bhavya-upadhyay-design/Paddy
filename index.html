Paddy Field

Each stem in this field is a famine. Rice is what people grow to stay alive, so here it stands for the lives that famine took.

Stem: it rises from the year the famine began. The taller the stem, the more years the famine lasted.

Grain: small, sparse grain means fewer deaths. Large, full grain means more.
