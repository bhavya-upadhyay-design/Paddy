Unharvested

Paddy is rice still in the field: grown, but not yet eaten. In Bengal in 1943, three million people starved while rice was still on sale in Calcutta. Each stem here is a famine; each head of grain, the lives it took.

“Starvation is the characteristic of some people not having enough food to eat. It is not the characteristic of there being not enough food to eat.”

Amartya Sen, Poverty and Famines, 1981

Stem: it rises from the year the famine began. The taller the stem, the more years the famine lasted.

Grain: small, sparse grain means fewer deaths. Large, full grain means more. Grain size also grows gradually within each band.

Death tolls are estimates. Hover any paddy for its figures.
